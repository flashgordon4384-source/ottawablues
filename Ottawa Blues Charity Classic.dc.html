01 / The Tournament

Fourteen years of first responders on the pitch.

The Ottawa Blues Charity Classic brings police, fire, paramedic, corrections and military personnel together for two days of indoor 7v7 — recreational and competitive divisions, round robin into playoffs, all under one roof.

Every dollar of proceeds goes to the National Capital Blues Foundation, funding mentorship, literacy and sport access for youth who need it most. To date the Classic has raised more than $90,000.
